## What You Can Do

### Code rights (governed by AGPL-3.0)

You may use, study, modify, and distribute the CARE-Y source code under the terms of AGPL-3.0. Nothing in this trademark policy restricts those rights.

### Permitted uses of the CARE-Y name

- **Factual references:** You may refer to CARE-Y by name in factual statements, such as "based on CARE-Y," "fork of CARE-Y," "compatible with CARE-Y," or "derived from the CARE-Y project."
- **Community discussion:** You may use the CARE-Y name in articles, blog posts, conference talks, documentation, and other commentary, including criticism.
- **Attribution:** You must retain attribution to the CARE-Y project as required by AGPL-3.0. Using the name for this purpose is permitted and encouraged.

### Unmodified redistribution

Organizations packaging unmodified official CARE-Y releases (for example, Docker images, VM appliances, or hosted trial environments) may retain the CARE-Y name, provided:

- The product or package is clearly marked as an unofficial or community build (for example, "Community Docker image of CARE-Y, not affiliated with CARE-Y Org").
- No claim of official status or endorsement is made.

### Self-hosted instances

Organizations running unmodified official CARE-Y releases for their own internal use may refer to their deployment as "CARE-Y" without restriction.

## What You Cannot Do

The following uses require prior written permission from CARE-Y Org:

- **Product names:** Using "CARE-Y" (or a confusingly similar variation) as the name of your product, service, or distribution of modified code.
- **Domain names:** Registering or using a domain name that includes "CARE-Y" in a way that could suggest official status or affiliation (for example, care-y.org, care-y-app.com, getcarey.io).
- **App store listings:** Publishing a modified fork to any app store under the CARE-Y name or a name that incorporates it.
- **Logos and branding:** Using the CARE-Y logo, wordmark, color scheme, or other branding elements in your product's UI, marketing, or communications.
- **Implied endorsement:** Describing your product or organization in any way that implies it is officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by CARE-Y Org.
- **Organization names:** Incorporating "CARE-Y" into a company name, nonprofit name, or other organizational identity.

## Forks and Modified Distributions

You are free to fork CARE-Y and distribute your modifications under AGPL-3.0. If you do, you must:

1. Give your fork a distinct name that does not include "CARE-Y."
2. Replace or remove the CARE-Y logo and any CARE-Y branding from the UI.
3. Make clear to users that your fork is not the official CARE-Y project.

You may still say "based on CARE-Y" or "forked from CARE-Y" in your documentation and release notes. That factual attribution is permitted and welcome.

---

## Why This Policy Exists

CARE-Y is a call intake and support system for at-risk populations. Users of CARE-Y deployments, including clients and volunteers, may rely on the CARE-Y name as a signal of the security and privacy standards built into the official software. Trademark protection ensures that a modified or unmaintained fork cannot trade on that signal in ways that could put vulnerable people at risk.
