# Acceptable Use Policy

CARE-Y is an encrypted call intake and support system built to protect at-risk populations from
state-level adversaries and other bad actors. This policy states the project's values and the
uses that are incompatible with those values.

## Prohibited Uses

The following uses are incompatible with the purpose of this project:

1. **Surveillance.** Deploying CARE-Y or any derivative work to conduct surveillance of the
   clients, callers, or volunteers the system is intended to serve.

2. **Encryption sabotage.** Modifying CARE-Y to weaken, bypass, disable, remove, or log
   around its encryption or anonymization protections.

3. **Misrepresentation.** Operating a CARE-Y instance that falsely represents its security
   properties to users (for example, claiming end-to-end encryption is active when it has
   been disabled or compromised).

4. **Facilitation of harm.** Using CARE-Y infrastructure to facilitate abuse, trafficking,
   exploitation, or other harm to the populations the software is designed to protect.

5. **Data commercialization.** Collecting, selling, licensing, or otherwise monetizing
   personal data about CARE-Y users, callers, or volunteers.

6. **Honeypot operation.** Running a CARE-Y instance disguised as a legitimate support
   service for the purpose of identifying, tracking, or entrapping users seeking help.

## Intent

CARE-Y exists because vulnerable people deserve infrastructure that works for them, not
against them. Every design decision (E2E encryption, the dual-tier encryption model with threshold
decryption for PII, zero plaintext at rest) exists to serve that goal.

Operators who share that goal are welcome to deploy, fork, and adapt this software.
Operators who do not are asked not to.
